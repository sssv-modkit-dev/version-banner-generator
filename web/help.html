BANNER LAB HELP

Make a release banner

01 / Bring your artwork

Simple View opens with Create and Batch tabs. Try the six original character sprites, or open static PNG/JPEG images. Transparent images work best. Images are read locally, stay in memory and are never uploaded. There is no analytics or browser persistence. Reloading clears the project.

Artwork and remixes

Browse 96 sprites at a time, choose Show all, or search by name or sheet. Choose Add to my composition to put several sprites together, then Edit layers to arrange them. Download cutout PNG saves transparent artwork on its own. Natural sizing keeps small sprites from being stretched too far.

Themes coordinate the banner. Advanced Style lets you change each text element's colour, size and lettering independently. Your overrides stay when you change themes; Follow theme clears one override.

The original sprite demo includes animated mashups. Pause the motion, scramble again, or choose a remix to keep editing. Downloads are still PNGs.

02 / Make your cuts

Opening an image suggests sprite crops using transparency or an estimated background. Pick a thumbnail to use in your banner. Find sprites again repeats detection; Adjust cuts opens Advanced View for corrections. Switching views keeps your work. Detection can split detached effects or join touching sprites. Flat sprite backgrounds connected to the image edges become transparent automatically. Turn off Automatically remove sprite background to keep the original. Busy backgrounds and checkerboards may remain. Use Rotate 90° or either flip button to orient your subject in either view.

In Advanced View, use a uniform grid for equal cells, custom guides for uneven rows, or free rectangles for an irregular atlas. Numeric fields use source pixels. Grid margins are symmetric; gutters are excluded. Counts calculate cell size by rounding down, with remainders controlled by “Include partial edge cells”.

Drag amber grid handles to resize cells, or drag custom guide lines. In rectangle mode drag to draw, drag inside a region to move it, or drag its lower-right handle to resize. Alt-drag draws over an existing region. Region selectors and numeric fields provide a keyboard alternative; arrows nudge a selected region (Shift for 10 pixels). Shift-drag pans. Click Apply cuts to update subjects. Undo/redo restores applied cuts. Overlapping rectangles are allowed and preserve duplicated pixels.

03 / Choose a subject

Select a thumbnail. Include/exclude checkboxes control batches and random selection. Rename it in Subject. Transparent-edge trimming is recorded alongside its original crop; fully transparent cells may be skipped.

For separate parts, create an assembly from the current subject and add cells with “+ Layer”. Layers are ordered back to front. Edit offsets, scale, rotation, visibility and flip in Subject. Each layer initially fits a 320 pixel box; alignment is manual. Banner placement controls transform the whole subject.

04 / Style it, then export

In Simple View edit your text and colour directly. Surprise me changes the subject and palette while keeping the text. In Advanced View, open Style for Classic, Reversed or Centered, a palette, background and matched quotes. Palettes change typography and accents; they do not recolour sprites. Disable smoothing for pixel art. PNGs use the same 1500 × 500 renderer as the preview.

Checked randomizer fields may change; unchecked fields are locked. Seed and shuffle index determine choices. Ordinary edits never reroll. Optional name lists use one name per line. Versions are always supplied by you.

05 / Make a batch

Choose one per included subject, seeded variations, or subjects × selected layouts × palettes. Style checkboxes set eligible pools. Random mode also respects Style locks. Preview, edit names, then download a ZIP of PNGs, recipes and a manifest. Numbered filenames stay stable after title edits. Rendering is sequential and cancellable; failed or cancelled jobs never download partial ZIPs.

Keep the recipe

In Advanced View save JSON alongside original source images. Open recipe restores cuts, assemblies, styles and resolved choices after checking SHA256 hashes. Re-select source images when prompted. Recipes contain no artwork or absolute paths. Browser exports use banner-lab-v1; PowerShell recipes cannot be replayed automatically here. Fonts and generator version are recorded; rasterization can vary between browsers.

Practical limits

Static PNG/JPEG only. 20 MB per file, 4096 pixels per side, 8 megapixels per image, 8 sheets and 24 megapixels per project. 1,024 regions per sheet, 1,124 subjects per project, 32 layers per assembly, 100 outputs per batch, 128 MB per ZIP. These are caps, not performance guarantees; reduce the batch on memory-constrained devices. Animated image export, anatomy detection, sprite recolouring and model rendering are not supported.

Requires modern Canvas 2D, Web Crypto and createImageBitmap support. Development checks cover Chromium and Firefox; Safari and touch devices require separate testing.
